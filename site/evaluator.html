How it works My home

Evaluate referee pairs for the NRC

Evaluators rate referee pairs on the IHF observation sheet, from game video or live, and the NRC returns the reports to the referees. Referees, club members and NRC members can hold the role once certified.

  • Rules exam.
  • Video calibration.
  • Certified. Write reports; the NRC reads each one before releasing it to the referees. Rules exam renewed every months, calibration every months.

Tournament organizers evaluate only once certified, like everyone else. You cannot evaluate a game you refereed; declare any other conflict (your own club, family) to the NRC.
