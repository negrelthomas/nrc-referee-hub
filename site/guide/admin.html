Quick learns

Short videos of each action, click by click, no sound. The people and events shown are examples.

Dashboard

  1. Sign in with your NRC email; you land on Admin. The red Admin button in the top bar brings you back from any page.
  2. The task band at the top lists what is due: the Sport80 export every 30 days, overdue programs, new contact requests.
  3. The figures: active referees, certified, clubs without a referee, clubs with a certified pair, new contact requests, referees without a baseline, programs in progress, programs overdue. Below, club coverage one row per club.
  4. The left menu is the map of the admin area. On a phone it becomes a row of buttons under the top bar.

Roster and contact requests

  1. Referees lists everyone. Search by name, club or airport; click a row to edit the record: photo, identity, phone, club, airports, level and certification date, Sport80 ID, compliance dates, admin notes, the Level confirmed by the NRC box (unticked shows an "NRC to confirm" badge on the public list) and Active.
  2. The record opens on the referee's latest evaluations (physical, theory, in game), their tournaments with divisions and games per year, and their tests. From the record you log an in game evaluation with the IHF observer criteria, log a game from the game sheet, Record physical test (Yo-Yo, sprint, agility) and Record theory exam (in person written or video exam). Online assessments count as theory automatically.
  3. On the public Referees page, every row opens a profile card: photo, level, club, tournaments and games per year. Signed in as admin, the card also shows the latest evaluations and an Edit profile button that opens the record here. Visitors never see evaluations.
  4. Referees edit their own name, phone, club, member ID, city, state, airports and photo under My profile. Their email is their sign in and only they change it (My profile > Change email): a confirmation goes to the current and the new address, and the change applies once both links are opened. The record here updates by itself. Before a referee's first sign in, the NRC can still correct the address in the record.
  5. Press Add referee for a new record. The email is the person's sign in; they can sign in the moment the record exists. Candidates start at level Candidate and get the Level 1 course automatically.
  6. Inquiries are the people who pressed "I want to be contacted" on the public page. Set the status as you work them (new, contacted, invited, closed). Add to roster opens the new referee form prefilled with their answers; saving it marks the inquiry invited.
  7. To remove someone, untick Active rather than deleting: their history and evaluations stay attached.

Staffing an event

Organizers submit the public request form; the NRC is emailed at once, the organizer gets an acknowledgment, and the request appears under Staffing and Tournaments. One event, one dialog with five tabs.

  • Staffing shows every tournament in a table (or cards): club and divisions, start date, deadline to staff with days left or late, what was requested, designated against needed, certified referees available that weekend, invitations sent, accepted, tentative, no answer, declined, and the last reminder. Filters: To staff, All upcoming, Past, All. Click a row to open the event.
  • Deadline and reminders: each request gets a deadline to staff (by default 42 days before the event; change it per event in its dialog). Until the event is staffed the NRC receives a reminder table every 7 days, then daily in the last 14 days before the deadline and once it is late. Press Change on the Staffing page to set the offset, the cadence and the address.
  • No duplicates: the database refuses a second request for the same event (same club or same name, overlapping dates) and tells the organizer which request exists. Older pairs that look alike are listed at the top of the Staffing page; cancel the one to drop.
  • Club required: every event is affiliated with a USATH registered club (international events excepted). Tick or untick Registered with USATH on each club under Clubs; only registered clubs appear on the request form.
  1. Open Staffing. To staff shows the upcoming events not yet fully designated, sorted by deadline. Cards switches to one card per event with a progress bar and the travel bookings status.
  2. Click a row. The header is editable: sanction, region, dates, venue, city, pairs, who books travel, hosting club, staff by date and divisions. Save stores it; Notify changes emails the designated officials and the organizer what changed (every change is logged).
  3. Find referees: certified referees ranked best first by declared availability for the event weekends (available, tentative, not declared; unavailable hidden by default), local airport, then level. Each row shows club, airport, games this season and an established partner. Tick the ones you want and press Add selected as draft invitations.
  4. Invitations: the drafts. Tick them and press Send invitations to selected. Each referee gets the email with accept, tentative and decline, and the same proposal on their home. The chips count draft, waiting, tentative, accepted, declined, confirmed against the number needed. Send reminder nudges the waiting ones (the hub also reminds automatically after 7 and 14 days). Remove draft deletes drafts not yet sent.
  5. Answers arrive by themselves with the date and the referee's note. Tentative referees can confirm from their home; you see it here.
  6. Confirm selected (accepted only): tick the accepted referees and confirm. The hub checks the count against the pairs requested and that an established pair stays together, and warns before going on. Confirming creates the assignment, emails each referee the full brief, moves the event to staffed, and offers to close the invitations still open with a short email to those referees.
  7. Notify organizer emails the organizer the designated list with a link to their organizer page and a private read only link.

Tournaments and clubs

  • Tournaments lists every event, requested or entered by the NRC, with sanction, divisions, dates, venue and organizer.
  • Add tournament records an event that did not come through the organizer form (NORCAL, nationals, international): name, sanction, region, dates, venue, games played, hosting club and divisions. Save and open goes straight to Officiated and games: add each referee and their games. This is what fills the referees' history and their public cards.
  • Divisions: Senior, U21, U19, U18, U17, U16, U14, U12, each men, women or mixed. Organizers tick them on the request form; you correct them in the event dialog.
  • Every event needs a hosting club registered with USATH (international events excepted). Under Clubs, open a club and tick or untick Registered with USATH; only registered clubs appear on the request form.

Designated and travel

  1. The Designated and travel tab lists the team with email, phone, home city and airport. The chips show referees designated against the need, supervisor or observer, and who books travel. Referees who answered accepted or tentative but are not designated are listed as open to officiate, in case of a withdrawal.
  2. Add an official directly: pick a name and a role (referee, supervisor, observer) and press Add. Use it for the supervisor the organizer asked for, an observer, or a last minute replacement.
  3. Add booking under an official records a flight, hotel, car or train: status, provider, reference, times, from, to, cost, who paid. The referee sees it on their home at once and can export it to their calendar; the organizer sees it on their page. Edit a booking with the edit link; delete it from the booking dialog.
  4. When the organizer books, they record their own bookings from their page; referees record what they booked themselves. Everything lands in the same table.
  5. Send brief to all designated sends the role specific confirmation (referee, supervisor with duties, observer) with the full event details and the travel arrangement. Send it again after a change.
  6. Remove takes someone off the team and reopens their invitation as declined, so you can pick a replacement from Find referees.

Expenses and the organizer report

  1. Expenses tab: the chips total bookings paid by the NRC, by the organizer, by referees, and claims submitted, approved and reimbursed. Each claim shows the official, type, description, amount, receipt (opens in a new tab), status and who reimburses (NRC or organizer).
  2. Press Approve or Reject, then Reimbursed when the money has gone out. The referee sees each status on their home; the organizer sees the totals.
  3. The organizer's post event report shows below the claims once filed: games played, ratings, highlights, incidents, would request again. Games per official feed the Officiated and games tab, which you can also fill or correct by hand.

Programs

  1. Programs holds the two settings cards. Certified referees: assessment questions (50), hurdle (80%), the per rule threshold that puts a rule into the program (80%), program length (12 weeks), study sessions per week, time limit, module questions and pass mark. Level 1 course: final test questions (30), pass mark (80%), time limit, question pool level, module questions and pass mark. Press Save on each card.
  2. The hurdle is cleared when the overall mark is reached and no rule sits below the per rule threshold; otherwise the hub builds a program on the weak rules and the referee re-tests at the end.
  3. Status by referee shows, for everyone on the roster: signed in or not, baseline taken, last score, program status and due date, modules done. Candidates show the Level 1 course progress and the theory passed date; you award Level 1 on the referee record after the practical part.

Training sessions

  1. Press Add session: title, type (online, clinic, course), audience, start and end, time zone, location, description, registration link, published.
  2. Published sessions appear on the public Training page under Next training sessions until they are past. Open a session to edit or delete it.

Questions

  1. The bank behind every quiz, course, assessment and program: the IHF Catalogue of Rules Questions 2025 plus the game situations deck. Filter by rule, level, source or text.
  2. Open a question to edit the wording, options, correct answers, explanation, rule reference or level, or set it inactive. Add question creates a new one. The Level 1 course and the Level 1 final test draw on Level 1 questions first and widen to the other levels when a rule has too few. The 49 questions with source NRC Level 1 were written for the demo and can be corrected or deactivated here.

Imports

  1. Referee roster (CSV): a spreadsheet saved as CSV with columns such as first name, last name, email, phone, club, city, state, airport, level, certified since, member ID, membership, SafeSport and background check dates. Rows match on email, then on name; new emails create a candidate record; empty cells keep the current value. The result lists created, updated and skipped rows.
  2. Sport80 membership export: the monthly export refreshes membership validity dates for every referee and clears the dashboard task.
  3. Import history keeps every run with its counts and skipped rows.

Automatic reminders

Every morning at 8am Pacific the hub checks:

  • Events not fully staffed: a table to the NRC every 7 days after the request, daily in the last 14 days before the deadline and once it is late (settings on the Staffing page).
  • Invitations unanswered for 7 days: one reminder to the referee, then one more a week later.
  • Designated officials without a booked flight or hotel within 21 days of an event the NRC books.
  • Organizers without a report three days after their event, and overdue programs.

Referees and organizers get their reminder once; the NRC gets one digest listing everything still open.
