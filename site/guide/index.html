User guide

How to use the hub

Three guides, one per role, each written click by click with screenshots. Pick yours. Every page can be read in ten minutes.

Referees

Sign in, keep your availability current, answer invitations, follow your trip, submit expenses, train and complete your program. Candidates: the Level 1 online course.

Tournament organizers

Request referees, follow the request from your own page, see who is designated and what was booked, edit or cancel, and report after the event.

NRC administrators

Roster, contact requests, staffing from invitation to confirmation, travel bookings, expenses, programs, training sessions, question bank and imports.

How a tournament gets staffed

The same sequence every time. Each arrow is one click or one email; nothing happens without someone deciding it.

Organizer submits the request→ NRC finds referees who are qualified and available→ Referees get an invitation: accept, tentative, decline→ NRC confirms the pairs→ Referees get the brief, the organizer gets the list→ Travel booked and tracked→ Event, then expenses and the organizer report

Signing in, for everyone

There is no password. Enter your email on the sign in page; the hub sends a link and a one time code. Click the link, or type the code on the sign in page if your mail app opened the link before you did. Both expire after one hour. Referees, organizers and NRC administrators use the same door and land on their own page.

Access is by invitation: referees are added to the roster by the NRC, organizers are known from the request they submitted, administrators are invited by the NRC. An email that is none of these gets a clear message and the NRC contact address.
