How a tournament gets staffed

From the organizer's request to the report after the event.

Organizer submits the request→ NRC finds referees who are qualified and available→ Referees get an invitation: accept, tentative, decline→ NRC designates the pairs→ Referees get the brief, the organizer gets the list→ Travel booked and tracked→ Event, then expenses and the organizer report
