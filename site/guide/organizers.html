User guide

Tournament organizers

How to request referees, follow the request, see who is designated and what is booked, change or cancel, and report after the event.

1. Request referees

  1. Open Request referees for a tournament. Submit at least 10 weeks before the event so flights stay affordable.
  2. Pick the USATH registered club hosting the event; a club not in the list writes to the NRC first. One request per event: if the event was already requested, the form says so and you change the existing request from your organizer page.
  3. Event: name, sanction (club, regional, USATH, international), region, dates, expected number of games, the total referee pairs the tournament requires and the pairs you source yourself outside USA Team Handball (for example from Canada or Europe, with their country or federation); the hub calculates the pairs USATH provides as the difference, and every US based referee comes through the NRC, earliest acceptable departure on the last day, and whether you need an NRC supervisor on site (required for USATH sanctioned events).
  4. Venue: name, address, city, state, nearest airport.
  5. What you cover: flights, hotel nights, airport transfers and how, hotel to venue transport, food stipend or meals. This is what the referees are told, word for word.
  6. Who books travel: you book directly with the referees, the NRC books and you reimburse, or each referee books their own travel and you reimburse what you cover. This decides who records bookings later.
  7. Contact: your name, email, phone, budget owner. The email is your sign in; use one you read.
  8. Press Send the request. The NRC is notified and you can sign in straight away to follow it.

2. Sign in

  1. Open the sign in page and type the email you used on the request. No password; you receive a link and a one time code.
  2. Click the link, or type the code on the sign in page. You land on My tournaments.

3. Follow the request

  1. Each request is a card. The strip at the top shows where it stands: Request received, NRC reviewing, Referees designated, Completed.
  2. The three figures: referees designated against the number you asked the NRC to staff, supervisor or observer, and who books travel. Below them: total pairs required, split between the pairs USATH provides and the pairs you source outside USATH.
  3. Pairs you source outside USATH are your pairs: the NRC designates USATH pairs only. Press Name a pair to record the two referees (federation, contact, home airport, how you reimburse them), or the NRC records them for you. They then appear with the team, with their bookings and expenses. Each referee with an email gets a sign in link to their own page with the event details, travel and expenses. To bring one more pair, edit the request first: the pairs USATH provides go down by one.
  4. Designated officials fills in as the NRC designates them: name, role, club, where they travel from, their email, and the travel bookings made for each of them. Until then it says the NRC is working on it.
  5. You also receive an email the moment the NRC designates the team, with the same list and a private link to a read only version of this page.

4. Edit, cancel, duplicate

  1. Edit the request opens every field. Press Save and notify: the changes are logged, the NRC and every designated official receive an email listing exactly what changed, and you get a confirmation copy.
  2. Cancel the event asks you to confirm, then records the cancellation and notifies the NRC and the designated officials. The NRC cancels the bookings it made.
  3. Duplicate as a new request opens the form prefilled with this event (venue, coverage, contact) for next year or the next edition. Change the dates and send.

5. Bookings and money

  1. If you book travel yourself, press Add a booking we made under the official concerned: kind, provider, reference, times, from, to, cost. The referee sees it on their home; the NRC sees it on the staffing dashboard.
  2. The Money line sums it up: bookings paid by the NRC that you reimburse, bookings you paid, bookings referees paid themselves, and expense claims submitted, approved and reimbursed. The NRC approves claims; you see the totals.

6. After the event

  1. Three days after the last day, the hub emails you a reminder and the card shows a Post event report button.
  2. Fill in games played, games per official, how the referees did (1 to 5), the supervisor if any, highlights, incidents, and whether you would request again. Press Send the report.
  3. Games per official feed each referee's record (tournaments and games officiated) and the NRC's view of who is active.
  4. From the first day of the event, the card shows Games and evaluations. Add each game with its referee pair (date, division, teams, score, video link if any), then press Evaluate next to each referee and score them on the USATH criteria. Evaluations go to the NRC; the NRC decides what the referee sees.
