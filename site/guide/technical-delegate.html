Technical delegate

The official who supervises the table, the substitution areas and the team officials. A different role from the timekeeper and the scorekeeper, with more authority and more accountability.

In development with the NRC. The duties below follow the IHF Rules of the Game (Rule 4, Rule 16, Rule 18, Clarification 7 and the Substitution Area Regulations). Who may act as a delegate at US events and how delegates are certified is for the NRC to decide. Practice quizzes: Training, Technical delegates.

2. Before the game

  • Check the table set-up: clock, signal, scoreboard, time-out cards and suspension cards.
  • Check the substitution areas and the coaching zone are marked as the Substitution Area Regulations require.
  • Check both team lists and the responsible team official of each team (Rule 4).
  • Brief the timekeeper and scorekeeper, then the referees: who interrupts for a faulty substitution, how suspensions and time-outs are signalled.

3. During the game

  • Supervise the timekeeper and scorekeeper; correct the clock or the sheet when they make a mistake.
  • Watch the substitution areas: faulty substitutions, extra players, team officials outside their area or the coaching zone.
  • Watch the team officials' conduct and inform the referees of unsportsmanlike conduct on the bench (Rule 16).
  • Keep the referees' attention on the court: use the next interruption for anything that can wait.

4. Interrupting the game

  • Interrupt at once for a faulty substitution or an illegal entry, and for violations in the substitution area (Clarification 7).
  • Explain to the referees what was seen. The referees decide the punishment and the restart, which depends on the reason and on who had the ball (Clarification 7).
  • Do not interrupt for a referee's judgment call on the court.

5. After the game

  • Check the score sheet before it is signed: score, team time-outs, every warning, suspension and disqualification with the minute.
  • Write the report on any disqualification with a written report and on any incident at the table or the benches.
  • Send the report to the organizing body; at US events, to the NRC.

6. At US events

  • The NRC designates technical delegates. On the hub, a delegate is designated on the event with the role Supervisor (technical delegate), separate from the referee pairs and from table officials.
  • Selection, certification and how delegates are evaluated are to be defined by the NRC.
