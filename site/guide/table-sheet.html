At the table: what matters most

For the timekeeper and the scorekeeper. Based on the IHF Rules of the Game; the referees take every decision. Keep it on the table during the game.

Before the throw-off

  • Test the clock, the horn or whistle and the scoreboard. Agree a backup for the time 2:3
  • Both team lists on the score sheet: up to 16 players and 4 team officials, responsible official marked 4:1, 4:2
  • Numbers on the sheet match the shirts
  • Three green time-out cards per team on the table
  • Brief with the referees: how you alert them, how you signal the end of a suspension

Timekeeper during the game

  • Start the clock on the referees' whistle for the throw-off. Stop it only on the referees' time-out signal or a team time-out 2:8
  • Team time-out: the team in possession puts its green card on the table. Whistle, stop the clock, show the time-out signal, note the minute. Three per team in regular time, two at most per half, one at most in the last five minutes 2:10
  • Suspension: two minutes of playing time from the restart. Goals do not shorten it. Tell the bench when the player may come back 16:3
  • Third suspension of the same player is a disqualification; the team still plays two minutes short 16:6, 16:8
  • Faulty substitution or illegal entry: interrupt at once and tell the referees what you saw 18:1, Clar. 7
  • Player not on the score sheet on the court: interrupt. A late player is approved by the table and written on the sheet before playing 4:3
  • No automatic signal: give the final signal at the end of each half 2:3

Scorekeeper: write it at once

  • Every goal: scorer's number and the running score
  • Every warning (yellow card): number and minute. One per player at most, three per team, one for the team officials together 16:2
  • Every 2-minute suspension: number and minute, players and officials
  • Every disqualification: red card, or red and blue when a written report is due. Number and minute 16:6, 16:8
  • Every 7-metre throw: thrower's number, scored or missed
  • Every team time-out: team and minute
  • Late players added; wrong numbers corrected

Half time and end of the game

  • Check the goals by player against the score; fix differences before the second half
  • Count cards and suspensions on the sheet against what the referees gave
  • Half time score and final score written
  • Sheet checked and signed as the event requires; disqualifications with a written report noted
  • Sheet handed to the organizer. It is the official result

Most frequent misses at US events

Cards and suspensions not written, 7-metre throws not recorded, goals credited to the wrong player. Write each card, suspension and 7-metre throw the moment the referee shows it, and check the scorers against the running score at every time-out and at half time.
