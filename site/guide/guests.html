Guest referees

For referees from outside the US named by a tournament organizer: your event, your travel, your expenses. About five minutes to read.

Who this is for

  • A pair from outside the US is always the organizer's pair: the organizer invites you and agrees your travel and expenses with you. USATH designates USATH pairs only.
  • The organizer or the NRC names you on the event on the hub. You then get an email with a link to your own page.
  • You are not evaluated by the NRC and nothing on the hub is mandatory for you.

Sign in

  • Click the link in the email, or open the sign in page, type the email the organizer gave for you and press Send me a link. There is no password.
  • The email holds a link and a code. Click the link, or type the code on the sign in page. Both work once and expire after one hour.
  • At your first sign in, a short form asks for a photo, your phone with country code, home city, the nation you live in, your closest airport, your level in your federation and how you are reimbursed. Press Save, or skip it and complete it later from Edit my profile. The organizer or the NRC can also fill it in.
  • Your email is your sign in: to change it, use Change email in your profile and confirm from both inboxes.

Your event

  • My events lists every event you are named for: dates, venue, the organizer's contact, the designated pairs and what the organizer covers.
  • You see your own events only, not the full calendar of USATH events.
  • If you can no longer officiate, press I can no longer officiate at this event and tell the organizer as early as possible.

Travel

  • Bookings made for you by the organizer or the NRC appear on the event with their reference and times.
  • Add a booking I made records travel you booked yourself: type, provider, reference, cost, times, from and to.
  • Add to my calendar downloads one file with the event and every booking, for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

Expenses

  • Press Submit an expense and attach the receipt, a photo or a PDF. The hub reads it and fills in the category, merchant, date, total and currency. Check each field, then Submit to the organizer. One claim per receipt.
  • Paid in euros or another currency: enter the amount and currency as on the receipt. The hub converts it to USD at the average ECB reference rate over the 30 days ending on the date of purchase. The claim keeps the original amount and the rate.
  • The organizer approves or declines each claim and pays it. The status shows under each claim, and a declined claim shows the reason.
  • How I get paid: your Venmo, PayPal or Zelle handle, seen only by the organizer who owes you. No bank account or card numbers on the hub; agree a bank transfer directly with the organizer.

Training

  • Training and tests opens the learning path, the free quiz, the flashcards and the rules assessment, all on the IHF Catalogue of Rules Questions.
  • Everything is optional: no deadline, no reminder, no ranking. Your scores are visible to you and the NRC only.
