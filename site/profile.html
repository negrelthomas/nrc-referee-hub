When I can referee

  • Tap a weekend to cycle: available, tentative, unavailable. A weekend covers Friday to Sunday
  • Events during the week are listed below the weekends: answer for each one, or add any dates under Other dates
  • Mark unavailable only the dates you cannot referee at all
  • Weekends of tournaments you are designated for show as Assigned: leave them, the NRC already counts on you

Events during the week

These events have game days from Monday to Thursday, which the weekend tiles do not cover. Your answer applies to all the event dates.

Other dates

Any period, weekdays included: available or tentative for a training camp or an event not listed, unavailable for travel or your club season. Unavailable dates win over everything else. Do not mark unavailable the dates of a tournament you are already designated for.

    A booking I made

    Welcome to the NRC Referee Hub

    This is where the National Referee Committee staffs tournaments and where you keep what the NRC needs from you. Five short screens, about two minutes.

    • Tell the NRC which weekends you can referee
    • Answer invitations and follow your trip, bookings and expenses
    • Train on the IHF rules and keep your certification current

    Your details

    USATH does not have these. They let the NRC plan travel and find referees close to an event. You can change them later on the Profile tab.

    Your availability

    On your home, tap a weekend to cycle it: available, tentative, unavailable. It saves on each tap. A weekend covers Friday to Sunday. Weekends left blank count as unknown, and the NRC considers declared dates first. Events with game days during the week are listed under the grid: answer Available, Tentative or Unavailable for each. Any other period, weekdays included, goes under Other dates.

    Invitations and trips

    When the NRC proposes you for an event you get an email with the dates, what the organizer covers and three buttons: Accept, Tentative, Decline. The same buttons sit on your home. Once the NRC designates the pairs, your trip card shows the venue, the bookings, a calendar file, and where to submit expenses.

    Change your email

    • We send a confirmation link to your current address and to the new one.
    • Open both links. Your email changes once both are confirmed.
    • From then on you sign in, and receive invitations and briefs, at the new address.
