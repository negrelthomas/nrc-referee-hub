Becoming a Level 1 referee

1

Contact the NRC

A short form. A committee member answers within a few days.

2

Online course

Six modules, about five hours in total, on your phone or computer.

3

Theory test

30 questions in 30 minutes. Pass mark 80%.

4

Clinic and first games

Half a day with the NRC, then games with an experienced partner.

What Level 1 takesOpen the Level 1 course

One certified pair in every club

The NRC's goal is a certified pair in every active USATH club. The club nominates two members, the NRC assigns a mentor and the training path, and the pair is observed at a tournament before certification.
