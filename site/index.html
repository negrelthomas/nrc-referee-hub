Why referees matter

The game only exists with them

Tournaments, leagues, collegiate championships and national team preparation all need certified pairs on the court. When a region has no referees, it has no sanctioned competition, and players stop progressing.

Quality on the court starts with the whistle

Fair, consistent and safe games come from referees who know the IHF rules, manage the game well and work as a pair. That standard is what the NRC trains, observes and certifies.

A real path, up to the international level

USATH certifies referees at Level 1, 2 and 3. The best go on to the continental level with NACHC and to the IHF. Every tournament you work is a step on that path, with feedback from the committee.

What the hub does

For referees

Keep your profile current (club, home base, airport, certification), train on the IHF rules in a few minutes a day, see your games and the feedback from observers, and respond to tournament assignments.

Sign in or start the certification path.

For tournament organizers

Submit your referee request once, with dates, venue and what you cover. The NRC selects the pairs and confirms them with you.

Request referees for a tournament

One certified pair in every club

The NRC's goal is simple to state: every active USATH club should have at least one certified referee pair of its own. Clubs nominate two members, the NRC assigns a mentor and the training path, and the pair gets observed at a tournament before certification.
