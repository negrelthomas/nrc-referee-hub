Become a referee

What Level 1 takes

About ten hours over four to six weeks, one clinic, and a committee that follows you from the first email to your first game.

Could you referee? Ten questions

Who can start

  • Anyone. No refereeing experience needed; playing experience helps.
  • USATH physical standards apply from 18 years old.
  • Officiated another sport? Say so in the form, the NRC can shorten the path.

Level 1 requirements and time

Requirements as published by USATH; durations are typical and depend on you.

Documents

About 2 hours
  • USATH referee membership on Sport80
  • SafeSport certificate (online course)
  • Background screening
  • All three must stay active every season; the hub shows their expiry dates on your profile

Rules: course and test

5 to 6 hours
  • Online course on this hub: six modules by rule group, about 45 minutes each
  • Study cards, then a 10 question test per module (70% to pass)
  • Final theory test: 30 questions, 30 minutes, 80% to pass
  • Study plan with calendar reminders, two sessions a week over three weeks

Practical: clinic

Half a day
  • Classes with an NRC member: whistle, hand signals, positioning
  • Shuttle run (beep test) at the standard for your age group
  • Level 1 certificate issued when everything above is complete

The steps

  1. Contact the NRC

    Fill in the short form on this page. A committee member answers within a few days, then adds you to the hub as a candidate.

  2. Sign in and complete your profile

    Club, home city, closest airport, the weekends you could work. Start the documents in parallel.

  3. Follow the Level 1 course

    Six modules with a test each. Pick a start date and the hub puts the study sessions in your calendar.

  4. Pass the theory test

    Opens once the six modules are done. Pass and "theory passed" is recorded on your profile with the date.

  5. Attend the clinic

    The NRC schedules the practical part and issues Level 1.

  6. Referee your first games

    Local events first, paired with an experienced referee. Observers record feedback on your profile.

After Level 1

Level 2

  • Level 1, documents active
  • Clinics and workshops during the season
  • Theory test, 75% to pass
  • Physical test at your age standard

Level 3 (National)

  • By NRC nomination: experience, dedication, observer evaluations
  • USATH sanctioned events, national and collegiate championships

Continental and international

  • NACHC Playing Rules and Referees Commission
  • IHF Global Referee Training Programme

One certified pair per club

  • A club with its own certified pair hosts sanctioned games and runs leagues without borrowing referees.
  • The club nominates two members; the NRC assigns a mentor and the training plan; the pair is observed at a tournament.
  • Ask your club board to nominate you, or use the form on this page.

Official source

Requirements are set by the NRC and published on the USATH certification page. If anything on this page differs, the USATH page prevails.
